## Scope Boundary

**TMD owns** brand awareness, messaging, social media, the newsletter and email marketing, lead magnets, the website and landing pages, graphics and event collateral, testing the handoff from the website to booking, and conversion tracking that carries no patient data.

**HOT owns** Jane or Tebra (Naita is comparing them), clinical intake, the Menopause Health Questionnaire, patient records and messaging, billing and insurance, scheduling administration, prescribing, care and clinical compliance.

**Rule:** No patient health information goes into marketing tools.

**Content approval:** Naita is the only reviewer of health-related content for medical accuracy and compliance.

## Proposed Patient Journey (to validate with Naita)

```
Awareness: social (Instagram first), newsletter, workshops, partners, LinkedIn
        │
        ▼
hotvi.org clinic page with a clear call to action
        │
        ├── A. Exploring (cold or warm): lead magnet opt-in, then a segmented nurture sequence
        │
        └── B. Ready (hot): short qualifier (3 to 4 questions, no health detail)
                      → book in Jane + non-refundable deposit
                      → Menopause Health Questionnaire sent after booking
                      → automated reminders until the form is complete
                      → in-person initial visit (45 to 60 min), then virtual follow-ups
```

What this means in practice:
- Patients book first and fill in forms afterwards, which lowers the barrier to booking.
- The deposit prevents no-shows.
- Leads are segmented as hot, warm or cold.
- Capacity is 4 to 6 in-person visits, one day a week (Thursdays) to start.
